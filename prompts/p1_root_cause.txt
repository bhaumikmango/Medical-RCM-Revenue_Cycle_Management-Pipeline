CARC 29 — Timely Filing:
- Filed late verdict is in rule_engine_flags. Do not recalculate.

  * Do not apply standard CARC 29 timely filing logic to this claim.
- Medicare: 365-day window. Commercial: 90–180 days.
- If delay_reason_code present -> recoverability = "needs_review".

CARC 16 — Missing Information:
- recoverability = "needs_review" always. The LLM assesses correction complexity.

- If claim_frequency = 1 and no pc_OrigRefNo -> needs_review. Request original claim reference from payer.
